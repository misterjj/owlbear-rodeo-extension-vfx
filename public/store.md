# Spellcast FX

**Make every spell land.** Fireballs that roar, lightning that jumps from foe to foe, walls of ice that stay on the map. The GM, and players if the GM allows it, cast animated effects in one click, and the whole room sees them.

👉 **[See every feature in action on the guide](https://vfx-owlbear.pages.dev/)**, and try all the effects right in your browser.

![Two ships trading cannon fire](https://vfx-owlbear.pages.dev/store/hero.jpg)

## Features

- **16 elements × 20 styles:** strikes, claws, arrows, projectiles, beams, cones, explosions, meteors, walls, domes, auras, chains, teleports and more.
- **One click** for explosions, domes, auras and healing. **Two clicks** for arrows, beams, cones and walls. **Chains** jump to every target you click.
- **Lasting effects** stay on the map or follow a token until you remove them, even for players who join later.
- **Token spells:** save up to 8 spells on a token, cast from a wheel by anyone who controls it.
- **Programs** recast effects on their own: a cannon firing every few seconds, a storm striking at random, braziers burning all night.
- **For the whole table:** players have nothing to install, effects stay hidden under fog of war, and the interface is in English and French.

## How to use

1. Open **Spellcast FX** in the top-left menu.
2. In the **Cast** tab, pick an element, a style and a size.
3. Click the map. An outline shows the area before it hits.

Check **Lasting** in **More options** to keep the effect on the map. To save spells on a token, select it and use **+ Add** in the Cast tab, then click the token and choose **Spells** during play.

![A fireball cast from a token's spell wheel](https://vfx-owlbear.pages.dev/store/token-wheel.jpg)

## Settings

The GM decides whether players can cast effects, what only the GM sees, and how many effects each scene can hold. Lasting effects keep using every player's graphics card, so lower the limits for slower machines.
