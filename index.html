I

Cast in one click

Open the panel, pick an effect, click the map. An outline shows the area before it hits.

  • One click for explosions, domes, auras, healing…
  • Two clicks for arrows, beams, cones and walls: from here, to there.
  • Chains jump to every target you click, until Esc or Finish chain.
  • Strikes and claws hit at random angles, or at the exact angle you set.

II

Effects that stay

Check Lasting, and the effect remains until you remove it, even for players who join later.

  • Drop it on a token and it follows: a burning aura, a shield, a curse.
  • Leave it on the map: a wall of fire, a dome of darkness, a poison cloud.
  • Arrows, claws and explosions leave a trace: a planted arrow, scorched ground.
  • The list shows who cast what. Hover ✕ to see it on the map before removing it.

III

Every hero, their own spells

Save up to 8 spells on a token. They follow it, and anyone who controls it can cast them from a wheel.

  • Prepare them once in the Cast tab, with + Add.
  • In play: click the token, then Spells. The fireball leaves from the wizard.
  • Buffs can stay on the caster or be aimed at an ally.

IV

Scenes that live on their own

Programs recast an effect by themselves: a cannon firing every 3 seconds, a storm striking at random, braziers burning all night.

  • Set the rhythm: random intervals, offsets, a number of shots.
  • Place points on the map or on tokens, in order or at random.
  • Group them and start a whole battlefield with one button.

Questions

Before you ask

Do players need to install anything?

No. Once the extension is enabled in the room, everyone sees the effects. They stay hidden under fog of war.

Can players cast effects?

Yes, if the GM turns on Players can cast effects in Settings. Token spells work for anyone who controls the token.

A new effect does not appear.

The simultaneous effects limit is probably reached. Raise it in Settings, or wait for running effects to end. Players and programs stop 2 short of the limit, so the GM can always cast.

Will it slow down my players?

Lasting effects keep using every player's graphics card. Limits per scene (10 by default) keep things smooth, and you can lower them for slower machines.

Do effects block vision?

No. They ignore walls and do not change vision rules. A darkness cloud is almost opaque, but only visually.

Which languages are available?

English and French. The interface follows the browser's language, and each player can change it in Settings.
